## Abstention ("not in the documents")

On the top vector similarity. "Best threshold" has the best balanced accuracy between answerable and unanswerable questions; the configured `min_score` instead sits just below the lowest answerable question. On-topic questions the documents do not answer (a German VAT rate, payroll in Orlavia) score as high as answerable ones, so a similarity threshold can only refuse clearly off-topic questions without losing answerable ones. It is a cheap first gate: the answer step must still say "not in the documents" when the passages do not answer, and the LLM evals measure that.
