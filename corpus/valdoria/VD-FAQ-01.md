# VCP frequently asked questions

## 1. From when must all companies clear their B2B invoices?
Large taxpayers clear their business-to-business invoices from 1 January 2026. All other VAT-registered companies must
clear their business-to-business invoices from 1 January 2027.

## 2. Do invoices to consumers need clearance?
No. Invoices to consumers are outside the scope of the e-Invoicing Act.

## 3. What happens when VCP rejects an invoice?
The invoice has not been issued. Correct the errors listed in the response and submit the invoice again within five
working days.

## 4. Where do I find the Clearance ID?
VCP returns the Clearance ID in its response to a successful submission. Keep it with the invoice; the technical
specification describes where it is stated on the invoice.

## 5. Can I correct a cleared invoice by issuing a new invoice?
No. A cleared invoice is corrected with a credit note that references the original invoice.
