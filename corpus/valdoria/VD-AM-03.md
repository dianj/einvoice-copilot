# Amendment A-2026/03 to the Valdoria e-Invoicing Act

## 1. Purpose
This amendment brings forward the date from which all VAT-registered taxpayers must clear their business-to-business
invoices through the Valdoria Clearance Platform (VCP).

## 2. Amendment to Article 4(3)
In Article 4(3) of the e-Invoicing Act, the words "from 1 January 2027" are replaced by "from 1 July 2026".
Consequently, business-to-business invoices issued by all VAT-registered taxpayers must be cleared through VCP from
1 July 2026. Invoices issued up to and including 30 June 2026 by taxpayers that are not large taxpayers do not need to
be cleared.

## 3. Transitional provisions
Large taxpayers remain obliged to clear business-to-business invoices since 1 January 2026; this amendment does not
change their obligations. Other taxpayers may clear their invoices voluntarily before 1 July 2026.

## 4. Entry into force
This amendment enters into force on the day of its publication.
