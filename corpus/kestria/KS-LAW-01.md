# Kestria Electronic Invoicing Regulation

## 1. Scope
From 1 September 2024, invoices between VAT-registered businesses (business-to-business) and invoices to public-sector
bodies (business-to-government) shall be issued and received as structured electronic invoices. Invoices to consumers
may continue to be issued on paper or as PDF documents.

## 2. Post-audit model
Invoices are not validated by the tax authority when they are issued. The Kestrian Tax Agency verifies invoices after
the fact, in audits, using the structured invoices kept by the supplier and the buyer.

## 3. KesNet exchange network
Structured invoices are exchanged over **KesNet**, a four-corner network: the supplier's software sends the invoice to
the supplier's access point, which delivers it to the buyer's access point, which passes it to the buyer. Every
participant is identified by a KesNet endpoint ID. Access points are certified by the Kestrian Tax Agency.

## 4. Archiving
Suppliers and buyers shall keep every structured invoice for ten years, in the original structured format in which it
was sent or received. A PDF rendering does not replace the structured invoice.

## 5. Currency
Invoices may be issued in any currency. When an invoice is not issued in Kestrian krona (KSK), the total VAT amount
shall also be stated in KSK, converted at the official exchange rate of the day before the tax point.

## 6. Public-sector buyers
An invoice to a public-sector body shall state the buyer reference that the body gave with its order.
