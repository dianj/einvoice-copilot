# Kestria VAT rates and category codes v2025

## 1. Rates
| Category | Meaning | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| S | Standard rate | 25 % |
| AA | Reduced rate (food, books, passenger transport) | 12 % |
| E | Exempt (financial services, health care) | 0 % |

## 2. Use in invoices
The rate of each VAT subtotal must be the rate of its category in this list (rule KS-05). A subtotal in category E must
state the exemption reason.
