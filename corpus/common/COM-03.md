# Country comparison overview (unofficial)

## 1. About this overview
This overview was prepared by a users' association for software vendors. It summarises the e-invoicing models of
Valdoria, Kestria and Orlavia. It is not an official document; the national laws and specifications prevail.

## 2. Valdoria
Clearance model: invoices are validated by the Valdoria Clearance Platform before they are issued. Business-to-
government invoices from 2025; business-to-business invoices of large taxpayers from 2026 and of all other taxpayers
from 2027.

## 3. Kestria
Post-audit model with the KesNet four-corner network. Business-to-business and business-to-government invoices are
structured since September 2024. Invoices must be kept for ten years.

## 4. Orlavia
Real-time reporting model: invoices are reported to the Orlavia Real-Time Transaction System after they are issued.
Business-to-business and business-to-government invoices are covered from 2025, with a reporting deadline of 24 hours.

## 5. Summary table
| | Valdoria | Kestria | Orlavia |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model | clearance | post-audit | real-time reporting |
| Government role at issue | validates before issue | none | receives data after issue |
| Main platform or network | VCP | KesNet | ORTS |
