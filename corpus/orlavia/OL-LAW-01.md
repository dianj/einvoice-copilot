# Orlavia Real-Time Invoice Reporting Decree D-4/2024

## 1. Scope
From 1 January 2025, every invoice issued by a VAT-registered person in Orlavia is subject to real-time reporting:
business-to-business, business-to-government and business-to-consumer invoices alike.

## 2. Reporting duty
The supplier issues the invoice directly to the buyer and reports the invoice data to the **Orlavia Real-Time
Transaction System (ORTS)** within 24 hours of the time of issue. Unlike a clearance system, ORTS does not approve the
invoice before it is issued; it receives the data and may query the supplier afterwards.

## 3. QR code
Every invoice, including invoices to consumers, carries a QR code that lets the buyer check the invoice against the
data reported to ORTS. The content of the QR code is defined in the ORTS reporting specification.

## 4. Self-billing
An invoice may be issued by the buyer on behalf of the supplier (self-billing) only when the two parties have
concluded a written self-billing agreement and the invoice references that agreement.

## 5. Corrections
A credit note is reported to ORTS in the same way and within the same deadline as an invoice.
