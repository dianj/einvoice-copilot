# Decree D-12/2025 amending the real-time reporting deadline

## 1. Purpose
This decree shortens the deadline for reporting invoices to the Orlavia Real-Time Transaction System (ORTS) set by
Decree D-4/2024.

## 2. New deadline
Invoices issued from 1 April 2026 shall be reported to ORTS within 4 hours of the time of issue. For invoices issued
up to and including 31 March 2026, the deadline of 24 hours remains.

## 3. Time of issue
The time of issue is the issue time stated on the invoice, in Orlavian local time. Invoices that do not state an
issue time are treated as issued at 00:00 of the issue date.

## 4. Entry into force
This decree enters into force on the day of its publication; the new deadline applies from 1 April 2026.
